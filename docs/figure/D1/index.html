Figure D1

Continued Use and Duration of Use of Adopted Heuristics

This figure shows the distribution of responses to Questions 1 and 2 of the follow-up survey (Online Appendix D.1). Panel (a) covers the heuristics identified in each respondent’s interview and shows whether the respondent still uses the heuristic and, if so, for how long (1,538 respondent–heuristic pairs). Panel (b) covers the heuristics that respondents no longer use and shows how long they had used them before stopping (166 pairs). In both panels, the Overall bar at the top pools all heuristics, and the column to the right of the bars reports the number of respondents behind each bar (for the Overall bar, the number of pairs). Heuristics are ordered from top to bottom by their prevalence in the full interview sample. Shares are printed inside the bars when they are at least 10%. Because we round these shares to whole percentages, they may not sum to 100%.
