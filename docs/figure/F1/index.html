Figure F1: Proportion of Investor Pairs with Non-Overlapping Heuristics

This figure shows the proportion of investor pairs whose two members share no heuristic. An investor invokes a heuristic if its score is at least three (“meaningful contributor”). We form all 1,185,030 unordered pairs of the 1,540 investors we interviewed and classify a pair as non-overlapping if its two members invoke no heuristic in common. Each bar conditions on the number of heuristics a focal investor invokes. The bar labeled k reports the proportion of non-overlapping pairs among all pairs in which at least one member invokes exactly k heuristics, and “4+” denotes four or more. A pair can enter more than one bar.
