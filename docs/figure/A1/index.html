Figure A1: Age, Gender, and Education of Respondents

This figure shows the distribution of responses to Questions 2 through 4 of the background survey (Online Appendix A.1). Each bar shows the share of the 1,540 interviewed investors selecting the answer choice, and the shares are printed above the bars. We omit the answer choice “Under 21,” which our screening excludes. One investor chooses to self-describe their gender.
