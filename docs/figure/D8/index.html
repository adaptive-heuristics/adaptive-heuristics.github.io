Figure D8

Portfolio Shares in Individual Stocks and in Local Stocks

This figure shows the distribution of responses to Questions 14 and 15 of the follow-up survey (Online Appendix D.1). Panel (a) shows the share of respondents’ overall investments held in individual stocks, and Panel (b) shows the share of their individual stockholdings invested in locally listed stocks. Each bar shows the share of the 600 respondents selecting the answer choice, and the shares are printed above the bars.
