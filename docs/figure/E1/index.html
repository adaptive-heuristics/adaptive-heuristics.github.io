Figure E1

Sensitivity of Belief Accuracy and Heuristic Delivery to Alternative Mappings

GPT-Based Mapping

Claude-Based Mapping

This figure compares the estimates under every possible map with those under our mapping. Panels (a) through (c) use GPT-6.1 Sol and Panels (d) through (f) use Claude Sonnet 5.5. Panels (a) and (d) show the belief-accuracy slope of Figure 8 of the main paper. The other panels show the delivery gap, which is the Adopted heuristics row minus the Alternative heuristics row of Table 7 (historical) and Table 8 (out-of-sample). As in the main paper, we standardize effectiveness within each objective, here separately for each map. Each small symbol is one possible distinct map, the large symbols are the consensus maps, and stars mark our mapping. Prompt A (dark circles) supplies no investor background, and Prompt B (light diamonds) adds the background of an investor who adopts the heuristic. Vertical offsets within each row only separate the symbols, and the dashed line marks zero.
