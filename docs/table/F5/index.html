Table F5: Follow-Up Survey Participation

This table compares the 600 investors who participated in the follow-up survey (Respondents) with the 940 investors who did not (Non-respondents). In each panel, Columns (1) and (2) report the group means, and Column (3) reports the difference between respondents and non-respondents. Panel A covers characteristics we measure at the interview stage. Its first row reports the number of investors in each group, and the next six rows report the percentage of investors in each group who are male (Male), are aged 35 or above (Age 35+), hold a bachelor’s degree or above (Bachelor’s or higher), report investable assets of at least USD 500,000 (High assets), report advanced or expert investment knowledge (Advanced knowledge), and report willingness to take above-average or substantial financial risks (Aggressive risk). Interview minutes is the elapsed duration of the interview in minutes, and Number of heuristics is the number of the thirteen heuristics the investor invokes. An investor invokes a heuristic if its score is at least three (“meaningful contributor”). Panel B reports, for each of the thirteen heuristics, the percentage of investors in each group who invoke it, and we order heuristics by their prevalence in the full interview sample. We test each difference with a two-sided two-sample t-test that allows unequal variances. Statistical significance at the 10%, 5%, and 1% levels is denoted by *, **, and ***, respectively.
