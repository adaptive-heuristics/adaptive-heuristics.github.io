Table F1: Interview Duration

This table examines whether the duration of the interview varies across investor demographics and countries. The dependent variable is the interview duration in minutes, the elapsed time between the first and the last message of the interview transcript, without winsorization. We estimate ordinary least squares regressions. Column (1) includes the six demographic indicators as independent variables, and Column (2) adds country indicators, with the US as the omitted category. The six demographic indicators are Male, Older (age ≥35), Higher education (bachelor’s degree or above), High wealth (investable assets ≥ USD 500,000), Advanced (self-reported advanced or expert investment knowledge), and Aggressive (self-reported willingness to take above-average or substantial financial risks). We demean them so that the intercept in Column (1) represents the unconditional sample average. We do not demean the country indicators, so the intercept in Column (2) is the fitted duration for a US investor with sample-average demographics. The sample comprises the 1,540 investors we interviewed. We report heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors in parentheses. Statistical significance at the 10%, 5%, and 1% levels is denoted by *, **, and ***, respectively.
