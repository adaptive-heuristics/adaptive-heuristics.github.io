Table F4: Objective Importance by Country

This table shows, separately by country, the percentage of follow-up survey respondents who consider each of four investment objectives important. On a five-point scale from 1 (“not important at all”) to 5 (“extremely important”), respondents rate how important each objective is to them personally when picking stocks (consistent high returns, high upside focus, stable performance, and personal values alignment). The first row, Overall, pools all 600 respondents. Each of the remaining ten rows corresponds to one country of residence. Column (1) reports the number of respondents in the country, and Columns (2) through (5) report the percentage of those respondents assigning a rating of 4 or 5 to the objective in the column header.
