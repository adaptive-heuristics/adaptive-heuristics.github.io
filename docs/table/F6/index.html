Table F6

Determinants of Objective Priorities

This table examines which financial objectives investors prioritize. We estimate linear probability models for each objective. The dependent variable, D(investor prioritizes the objective), is a dummy variable equal to one if the investor rates the objective strictly above her own average importance rating across the three financial objectives. The six demographic indicators are Male, Older (age ≥35), Higher education (bachelor’s degree or above), High wealth (investable assets ≥ USD 500,000), Advanced knowledge (self-reported advanced or expert investment knowledge), and Aggressive (self-reported willingness to take above-average or substantial financial risks). Mkt excess return, Avg skewness, and Avg volatility are country-level averages over July 2015 through June 2025, the ten years preceding our interviews, of the annualized value-weighted market excess return and the value-weighted means of the 21-day realized skewness and volatility of individual stocks. We compute all three from the data of Jensen, Kelly, and Pedersen (2023) and standardize them across the ten countries. For each objective, the first column includes the three market characteristics, and the second replaces them with country indicators, with the US as the omitted category. All six columns include the six demographic indicators and a constant. We report heteroskedasticity-robust standard errors in parentheses. Statistical significance at the 10%, 5%, and 1% levels is denoted by *, **, and ***, respectively.
