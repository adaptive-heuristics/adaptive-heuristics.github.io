Table F13

Do Adopted Heuristics Deliver on the Objectives Investors Do Not Prioritize? Out-of-Sample Evidence

This table repeats the out-of-sample analysis of Table 8 of the main paper on the objectives investors do not prioritize. We recompute delivery on outcomes realized after our interviews. The out-of-sample period covers portfolio sorts from September through November 2025, and we measure delivery over the month following each sort, so that all outcomes fall between October and December 2025. Because that period is too short to estimate a market beta, we calculate the CAPM alpha for the returns objective with the market beta fixed at its estimate from the preceding ten years (September 2015 through August 2025). We standardize each outcome within objective across the seventy heuristic–country pairs, recomputed on the out-of-sample window. An objective counts as non-prioritized if the investor rates it strictly below her own average across the three financial objectives. The adopted heuristics, the aggregation, and the samples are identical to Table F12. The two benchmark rows replace the adopted rule set of each investor with an equally sized random draw from (i) the covered heuristics she does not adopt (Alternative heuristics) and (ii) all seven covered heuristics (Randomly selected heuristics). These two rows report means across 10,000 draws, with standard deviations across draws in parentheses, and their p-values are the one-sided fraction of draws that deliver at least as much as the adopted set. Table F17 reports the average-stock and JKP-factor-based portfolio benchmarks, with the same adopted-heuristics row. Statistical significance at the 10%, 5%, and 1% levels is denoted by *, **, and ***, respectively, referring to the p-values in the brackets.
